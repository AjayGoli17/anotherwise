SHAKTI KINETIC · Case study

Gym trainer platform

Every client trains differently. This platform lets a trainer build workout and diet plans, assign each one to a single client, and see what they actually did.

Year
2026
Status
Concept build · demo data
Services
Custom Software · Business Systems
Our role
Design & full-stack build

The challenge

Every client needs a different plan. Most coaches send them as chat messages and spreadsheets, then have no idea what was followed.

Our approach

One workspace to build workout and diet plans and assign them to each client, and a phone app where clients follow them and log every session.

The outcome

Every client gets a plan made for them, and the coach sees exactly what got done.

One plan never fits everyone.

  • Plans are personal: A copied plan stops fitting the moment a goal, level or diet differs.
  • Plans get lost in chats: A workout pasted into a message gets buried, and nobody updates it.
  • Diets take hours: Calories, macros and meal times are slow to work out by hand for every client.
  • No proof of follow-through: Without a log, the coach only knows what happened by asking.
  • Admin steals coaching time: Sessions, progress and payments sit apart from the plans they belong to.

From a blank plan to a followed plan.

  1. Build a plan once: Workout plans list every exercise with sets, reps, load, rest and a coaching cue. Diet plans start as templates with calories, macros and timed meals, and the form blocks impossible targets before they’re saved.
  2. Assign it to one client: A workout plan becomes that client’s active plan. A diet template is copied into their own plan, so changing it for one person never touches the template or anyone else.
  3. Get the log back: Clients check off exercises, rate effort, add notes and log meals from their phone. All of it lands in the record the trainer reads.
  4. Judge the plan by results: Check-ins, 1RM lifts, sessions, progress photos and billing live beside the plans, so you can see whether a plan is working.

Plan to proof, in five moves.

Workout or diet, the path is the same, and it ends with the trainer seeing what the client really did.

  1. Build · Plan or template · Exercises, or calories, macros and meals
  2. Assign · One client · Their own copy, ready to adjust
  3. Deliver · Client’s app · The plan shows up on their phone
  4. Follow · Log it · Exercises, effort, notes and meals
  5. Review · Trainer view · Volume, consistency and progress

Results

  • 2 Plan types, assigned per client
  • 7 Trainer workspaces in one dashboard
  • 219 Automated tests, all passing

What we learned

  • Template once, tailor per client: Tailoring one client’s diet never changes the template or anyone else’s plan.
  • Protect the coach’s work: Clients see the plan as prescribed but can’t rewrite it, and every delete is ownership-checked on the server.
  • Logging in one tap: Mobile-first, so checking off a set or logging a meal takes a tap, not a message.

Built with

  • Node.js + Express
  • Vanilla JavaScript front end
  • PostgreSQL-ready, with in-memory demo data
  • Chart.js progress charts
  • Role-based sign-in (trainer / client)
  • Private, revocable client links
  • Photo upload with validation
  • Automated test suite
